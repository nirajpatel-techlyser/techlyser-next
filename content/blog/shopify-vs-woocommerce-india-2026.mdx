Choosing between **Shopify** and **WooCommerce** still comes up in almost every Indian D2C discovery call. The wrong answer costs months of rebuild later.

This comparison is written for Indian operators — COD, UPI, festive peaks, GST ops, and agency bandwidth included.

## Snapshot comparison

| Factor | Shopify | WooCommerce |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosting | Managed SaaS | You (or host) manage WordPress |
| Speed to launch | Usually faster | Depends on theme/plugins |
| Payments / UPI | Strong app + gateway ecosystem | Flexible, more DIY |
| COD workflows | Apps + custom flows | Highly flexible |
| Total cost of ownership | Platform fee + apps | Hosting + plugins + maintenance |
| Security updates | Platform-managed core | You own WP/plugin patching |
| Best fit | Focused commerce teams | Content-heavy + custom PHP needs |

## Where Shopify wins for Indian D2C

1. **Operational focus** — less time babysitting servers  
2. **App ecosystem** for subscriptions, reviews, loyalty, logistics  
3. **Predictable theme architecture** when built by a senior team  
4. **Scaling path** to [Shopify Plus](/shopify-plus-vs-shopify-indian-brands)  
5. **Agency market depth** — easier to hire specialists ([Shopify developers India](/shopify-developers-india))

## Where WooCommerce still wins

1. **Content + commerce** on one WordPress CMS your editors already know  
2. **Deep PHP customization** without Storefront API constraints  
3. **Certain niche B2B** plugins already embedded in your ops  
4. **Lower platform fees** if you have strong in-house WP talent  

If your “store” is really a content magazine with a cart, Woo can be rational. If your business is **selling products**, Shopify usually compounds better.

## Total cost of ownership (TCO) — think 24 months

### Shopify TCO includes
- Plan fee  
- Apps (often the silent budget killer)  
- Theme/custom development  
- Payment processing rates  

### WooCommerce TCO includes
- Hosting (and upgrades when traffic spikes)  
- Premium plugins  
- Security, backups, staging  
- Developer hours for breakage after WP updates  
- Performance firefighting  

Indian brands often underestimate Woo **maintenance** and underestimate Shopify **app sprawl**. Both need governance.

## SEO & speed realities

Neither platform ranks by default.

- **Shopify** can be extremely fast with a lean theme; app bloat kills CWV.  
- **Woo** can be fast on good hosting with a custom theme; plugin stacks often destroy LCP.

## Decision checklist

Choose **Shopify** if:

- Commerce is the core business  
- You want managed infrastructure  
- You may need Plus within 24 months  
- You value specialist Shopify partners  

Choose **WooCommerce** if:

- WordPress content workflows are non-negotiable  
- You have strong WP engineering in-house  
- Custom PHP is a competitive advantage
